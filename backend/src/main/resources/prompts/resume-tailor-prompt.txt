INCLUDE:

DECISION TEST: Before adding any keyword ask — "Would a recruiter type this exact term into
an ATS search box to filter candidates?" If NO, exclude it.

STEP 2 — GENERATE THE TAILORED RESUME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Using the candidate profile as the foundation, generate a complete ATS-optimized resume.

CRITICAL GENERATION RULES:

1. ENRICH FREELY — You are ALLOWED and ENCOURAGED to:
   - Add relevant technical skills the candidate likely has based on their background,
     even if not explicitly listed in their profile (e.g. if they use React, they likely
     know JavaScript, HTML, CSS, npm — add them)
   - Enhance experience bullet points with inferred technical details that are realistic
     and consistent with the candidate's actual roles and seniority
   - Add industry-standard tools a person in their role would realistically use
   - For student/entry-level profiles: generate a strong skills section drawing from
     coursework, projects, and adjacent technologies even if not explicitly mentioned

2. DO NOT FABRICATE — Never invent:
   - Company names, job titles, or employment dates
   - Degrees, institutions, or graduation years
   - Certifications not held

3. KEYWORD SATURATION — Every keyword in extractedKeywords MUST appear verbatim
   somewhere in the resume (summary, experience bullets, or skills section)

CHARACTER TARGETS (for ATS density and readability):
  - Professional Summary : 300–450 characters
  - Total Experience     : 2800–4000 characters across all roles
  - Skills Section       : 450–650 characters

A. PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY (2–3 sentences, no "I"):
   - State the exact job title from the JD verbatim as the opening descriptor
   - Mention the candidate's primary aligned technologies
   - Reference key methodologies/practices (CI/CD, Agile, full SDLC, etc.) if relevant

B. EXPERIENCE SECTION:
   Recent/current role  → 6–9 bullets, 1000–1400 chars
   Mid-level role       → 4–6 bullets,  700–900  chars
   Older / early role   → 3–4 bullets,  500–750  chars

   Every bullet must:
   - Start with a strong action verb (Built, Designed, Led, Optimized, Implemented, Reduced...)
   - Include at least one extractedKeyword naturally
   - Quantify impact where defensible (%, latency improvements, scale, user count, time saved)

C. SKILLS SECTION (categorized):
   Use categories: Languages | Frameworks | Databases | Cloud & DevOps | Tools | Methodologies
   - Every extractedKeyword defensible for this candidate must appear here or in experience
   - Add adjacent/inferred skills that strengthen the candidate's profile for this role
   - Use exact wording from the JD for keywords

D. EDUCATION & CERTIFICATIONS:
   Copy verbatim from candidate data — do not modify dates, institutions, or degree names.

STEP 3 — SCORE THE RESUME (YOUR EXPERT JUDGMENT)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

After generating the resume, act as an ATS system + senior recruiter and score it.

SCORING CRITERIA — weigh each factor:

  Technical Keyword Match (40%)
    How many of the extractedKeywords appear in the resume?
    Count exact matches. matchedKeywords = keywords present in the generated resume.
    missingKeywords = extractedKeywords NOT present in the resume.

  Candidate-Role Fit (25%)
    How well does the candidate's actual background align with the role requirements?
    A perfect keyword match with zero relevant experience should not score 100.
    A student applying for a senior role should score lower on fit even with good keywords.

  Resume Completeness & ATS Format (20%)
    Does the resume have all critical sections (summary, experience, skills, education)?
    Are sections properly structured for ATS parsing?
    Is experience relevant and clearly described?

  Keyword Density & Placement (15%)
    Are keywords distributed across summary, experience, AND skills?
    Or are they only stuffed in the skills section?
    Natural integration across sections scores higher.

SCORING RULES:
  - Score range: 0–100 (integer)
  - Be realistic and honest — a junior candidate applying for a senior role should score 45–60
    even with good keyword matching, because fit matters
  - A strong match with relevant background should score 80–95
  - Do not inflate scores — users depend on this to understand their real competitiveness
  - Provide matchedKeywords and missingKeywords accurately

Score labels:
  90–100 : "Excellent"
  75–89  : "Good"
  55–74  : "Fair"
  0–54   : "Needs Improvement"
